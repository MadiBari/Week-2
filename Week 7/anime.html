My Favorite Animes

My top three anime shows in no particular order are Full Metal Alchemist Brotherhood, Frieren: Beyond Journey's End, and Delicious in Dungeon. Each of theses shows explores different aspects of what it means to be human, Full metal handles humanities struggle with confronting divinity and the meaning of the universe. Frieren shows us that a vital part of the human experience is the memories and experiences you have with other people. Delicious in Dungeon while originally appearing to be a light hearted cooking show explores what primal hunger really is, for both sustanance and power, and simultaneously understanding our spot in a larger food chain and how our actions directly effect the world around us wether we mean for them to or not.

Many people think of anime as surface value entertainment but I find that in the story telling these shows utilize art, music, and some of the most beautiful composition to explore these themes, in a way that many other forms of media struggle to. Some may say it's not that deep but once a piece of literature leaves the hands of an artist it is up to the audience to interpret the meaning as they see fit. Not to justify their own point of view but to expand their horizons.

Take a peak at these shows for yourself!
